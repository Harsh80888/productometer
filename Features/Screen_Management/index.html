Unlock Restricted Settings

On Android 13, 14, and 15, accessibility services in sideloaded APKs are automatically greyed out as "Restricted setting". Follow these 4 quick steps to unlock:

1 Tap Open App Info below to go directly to Productometer's phone settings.
2 In the top-right corner of the system page, tap the 3 vertical dots (⋮) menu.
3 Select "Allow restricted settings" and authenticate with your fingerprint or PIN.
4 Return here and tap Enable Focus Shield → toggle the Accessibility switch to ON!
